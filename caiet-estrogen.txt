The reason I was asking during which generation you were born was that I'm from the generations during which preparatory school was optional. Consequently, I was put directly in the first grade at an age when otbers were in preparatory school. That's the way everyone went about this until a few years later. Some of us had neat handwriting, some had not.

It is true that I think that boys were more likely to write in hieroglyphs, but I am a 'girl' and I have always had bad hand handwriting (although i've also had cognitive delays due to eyesight problems and autism).

The study you mentioned might play a part in this, but (and please excuse the sociology major in me) I'd argue that society has a greater effect.

Girls will be admonished more often than boys regarding their handwriting, because neat handwriting is 'feminine' (during middle school, my music teacher made me rewrite my whole notebook for a ten, it was not 'feminine' enough...).

Boys can pretty much write whatever they want; though teachers will still complain that they're unable to read it, there isn't the same kind of pressure girls are put under.
</anon>
